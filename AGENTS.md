# Topical Knowledge Cell Enterprise

This package is the generic carrier for one sovereign topical knowledge-cell
enterprise. An instance owner admits its own topic, source-projection receipts,
disclosure policy, custody assignment, and hired provider bindings. This
package contains no topic-specific content or provider implementation.

The cell owns its definition and receipts. It hires separate services for
Leader-authorized notebook creation, NotebookLM, corpus reconciliation,
discovery, reports, brokerage, RMN identity, and RWIL/RDF durability. Optional
domain services are owner-selected and never imported. The carrier does not
crawl, create, mutate, generate, publish, pay, select suppliers, or copy a
supplier interior.
